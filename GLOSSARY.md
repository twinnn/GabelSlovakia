# Gabel SK & CZ

The official website of the Gabel pole distributor for Slovakia and the Czech Republic, serving both consumers and business customers.

## Language

**Distributor**:
AC ecom s. r. o., the company holding the official Gabel distribution rights for Slovakia and the Czech Republic.
_Avoid_: Gabel Slovakia, importer, Gabel SK, AcEcom

**Gabel**:
The Italian pole manufacturer whose products the Distributor sells; its own brand rules and approvals apply to the site.
_Avoid_: Gabel Italy, Gabel Sport, the brand

**Market**:
One of the two countries the Distributor serves — Slovakia or the Czech Republic — each with its own language, contacts and partners.
_Avoid_: Region, country version, locale

**Market site**:
The Distributor's website for one Market, in that Market's language only: www.gabelpalice.sk for Slovakia, www.gabel.cz for the Czech Republic.
_Avoid_: Language version, mutation, subsite

## Audiences

**Consumer**:
A private person who uses Gabel poles and buys them from a Dealer; the site's primary audience.
_Avoid_: B2C, end customer, user

**Dealer**:
An independent business that buys Gabel poles from the Distributor and resells them, online or in a store (e.g. go-noow.sk, Sportissimo).
_Avoid_: Shop, e-shop, reseller, B2B partner

**Rental**:
A business that rents Gabel poles to Consumers.
_Avoid_: Rental shop, požičovňa partner

**Club**:
A sports club that buys Gabel poles for its members or racers.
_Avoid_: Team, ski club partner

**Partner organisation**:
A sports federation or association the Distributor cooperates with (e.g. ZSL, SFNW, ČMFNW); it does not buy or sell poles.
_Avoid_: Partner, sponsor

## Products

**Category**:
One of the four sports Gabel poles are grouped by: Nordic Walking, Trekking, Trail Running and Ski (alpine).
_Avoid_: Sport, segment, collection, product group

**Product line**:
A named family of poles inside a Category, such as e-poles inside Nordic Walking.
_Avoid_: Category, series, collection

**Catalogue**:
Gabel's seasonal product brochure for one or more Categories, published by Gabel in EN/IT/DE (e.g. Snow Winter 26/27).
_Avoid_: Brochure, katalóg (as a page name)

## Programmes

**Gabel4Youth**:
The Distributor's support programme for young Slovak ski racers, with a seasonal ranking; runs in the Slovak Market only.
_Avoid_: Gabel pre mládež, youth programme, Gabel 4 Young

**Price-list**:
The Distributor's wholesale prices, given only to Dealers, Rentals and Clubs on request; Consumer-facing pages carry no prices.
_Avoid_: Cenník (on public pages), RRP
